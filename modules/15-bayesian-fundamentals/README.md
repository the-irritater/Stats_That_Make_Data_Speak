# Module 15: Bayesian Statistics Fundamentals

*Classical statistics assumes parameters are fixed and data is random. Bayesian statistics flips this, treating data as fixed and parameters as probability distributions.*

---

## Before You Begin

Before starting this module, you should have a solid understanding of:
- **Conditional Probability (Module 3):** Joint probability, independence, and conditioning.
- **Bayes' Theorem (Module 3):** The base formula for updating beliefs.

---

## Why It Matters

Bayesian methods allow data professionals to continuously update decisions as new observations arrive. Unlike frequentist methods, they don't require large samples to begin making assertions, and they natively represent uncertainty as intuitive probability ranges. This makes them highly valuable for online experimentation, recommendation systems, personalization engines, and financial risk models.
