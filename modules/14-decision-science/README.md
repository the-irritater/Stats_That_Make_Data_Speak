# Module 14: Modern Analytics & Decision Science

*Statistics and models are useless if they don't change decisions. Learn how to design KPIs, analyze retention, and communicate insights to executives.*

---

## Before You Begin

Before starting this module, you should have a solid understanding of:
- **SQL Basics:** Writing SELECT queries, aggregation, and filtering.
- **Python:** Basic data manipulation and plotting.
- **Visualization:** Basic chart types (bar, line, scatter).
- **Statistics (Modules 1–13):** Hypotheses, metrics, and regression.

## The Big Idea

You can write queries, build regression lines, and run forecasting models. But if your manager cannot understand your slide deck, or if your metrics don't lead to a strategic decision, your analysis has failed. This final module bridges the gap between technical execution and business value.

> Data alone does not speak. Statistics makes it speak. Decision science makes it matter.
