## The Big Idea

Module 9 asked *"Is there a difference?"* This module asks *"Can we explain and predict the outcome?"* Regression is the single most important tool in a data analyst's toolkit — it quantifies relationships, controls for confounders, and makes predictions.

Three key areas:

1. **Building the Model** (Day 71–74) — from simple to multiple regression, including categorical predictors and interactions
2. **Diagnosing Problems** (Day 75–77) — multicollinearity, model selection, and regularization
3. **Extending to Classification** (Day 78–80) — logistic regression and a full case study

> If you learn one statistical method deeply, make it regression. Every other technique is either a special case of it or a competitor to it.
