# Module 12: Experimental Design & Causal Inference

*Correlation tells you that two things move together. Causal inference tells you which one pushed the other — and by how much.*

---

## Before You Begin

Before starting this module, you should have a solid understanding of:
- **Hypothesis Testing (Module 5):** Rejection regions, p-values, Type I and II errors.
- **Confidence Intervals (Module 5):** Estimating ranges of effect sizes.
- **Regression (Module 6 & 10):** How control variables isolate associations.

## The Big Idea

Data analysts are routinely asked: *"Did our change cause this increase in sales?"* Most analysts point to a chart where the metric went up after the launch. This module explains why that is a critical mistake, and teaches you the experimental and observational methods needed to prove causality.

> Proving causality is the highest-value skill a data professional can possess.
